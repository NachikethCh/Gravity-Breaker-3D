GRAVITY BREAKER — v2 NEON COURSE / VARIED HAZARDS / STAMINA BOOST

This update builds on the tested 1500 m attack-telegraph build. It keeps the
existing menu, audio, scoring, lives, checkpoints, gravity flip, projectiles,
telegraph warnings, victory/game-over flows, and saved best score.

VISUAL COURSE UPDATE
- Replaced the plain single-color surface treatment with alternating metallic
  cyan/indigo panels across the floor, ceiling, and both walls.
- Increased the visibility and thickness of the neon graph lines.
- Added cyan/purple wall-side light ribs every 30 m.
- Darkened and metallic-treated the base tunnel materials, and increased glow.
- Panel rendering uses MultiMesh batches to keep the draw-call count low.

OBSTACLE UPDATE
- Spikes grow from 1.35x near the beginning to 2.25x near the finish.
- Three out of every four laser gates now move in two axes.
- Moving lasers use two independent axes: floor/ceiling gates slide in X and
  bob in Y; wall gates slide in Y and drift in X.
- Added 12 special hazards from 245 m onward, spaced throughout the course:
  * Oversized moving piston crushers (about 2.8 m wide and tall).
  * Rotating three-blade fans.
  * Floating rotating energy mines.

SPEED / STAMINA UPDATE
- Hold W to boost from the normal 6 units/s toward 12 units/s (2x speed).
- Boost drains stamina; a full tank supplies about 4.3 seconds of boost.
- Release W to recover stamina at 18 points/s; boost unlocks again at 30%.
- Hold S to brake toward 2.5 units/s.
- Shift and Ctrl remain alternate boost/brake keys.
- HUD now shows speed and a live stamina bar.

CONTROLS
A / D       Strafe
SPACE       Jump
Q           Flip gravity
W           Boost (stamina-limited)
S           Brake
SHIFT       Alternate boost
CTRL        Alternate brake

TESTING NOTE
The files were statically checked and the ZIP archive was integrity-tested.
A Godot 4.7 executable is not available in the build environment, so this
version has not been launched or play-tested here. Open the project in Godot
4.7 and verify the visuals, collisions, motion, and controls in-engine.
