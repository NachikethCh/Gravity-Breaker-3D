GRAVITY BREAKER — v7 visual refinement
========================================

Base project: v6_grid_bootstrap_fix

What changed:
- Kept the working v6 grid-generation bootstrap and its GeneratedRunnerMarkings root.
- Changed the procedural grid from 1 m spacing to clear 2 m x 2 m cells.
- Kept minor grid lines subtle; added stronger cyan lines every five cells (10 m)
  and muted violet accents every ten cells (20 m).
- Tuned line widths and colours down to reduce the bright wireframe/cage appearance.
- Darkened the four tunnel surface materials to a restrained navy / blue-grey palette.
- Disabled emission on the large tunnel surfaces so the walls and floor do not self-illuminate.
- Moderately reduced ambient lighting, directional light energy, and global glow/bloom while retaining enough fill light to keep the player and panel faces readable.
- Left obstacle materials and obstacle/gameplay logic unchanged.
- Preserved the 1500 m course, stamina boost/brake, 2 s gravity-flip cooldown,
  menus, audio, scoring, checkpoints, lives, enemy projectiles, and attack telegraphs.

Expected appearance:
A dark metallic training shaft with readable rectangular graph cells. The grid should
be visible but not overpower the player or hazard silhouettes. Cyan is used for the
10 m major divisions; low-contrast lines define the 2 m cells; violet accents mark
20 m sections.

Validation:
- ZIP archive integrity checked after packaging.
- Source changes inspected and checked for expected constants / material settings.
- Godot 4.7.2 executable was not available in the build environment; no in-engine
  rendering or collision test is claimed. Test this build in a separate folder first.
