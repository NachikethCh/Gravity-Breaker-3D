GRAVITY BREAKER v10 — FASTER PROGRESSIVE SPEED

Built on the v9 progressive-speed / v8 sector-progression project.

Speed tuning:
- Normal auto-run increases continuously from 9.0 to 14.0 units/s over 1500 m.
- W / Shift boost scales with current cruise speed using a 1.65x multiplier,
  capped at 22.0 units/s.
- S / Ctrl braking now bottoms out at 4.0 units/s.
- Boost acceleration: 11.0 units/s^2.
- Braking deceleration: 13.0 units/s^2.
- Return-to-cruise response: 7.0 units/s^2.
- Lateral strafe speed: 6.0 units/s.
- Run animation speed scales with movement speed (0.65x to 2.0x).

Preserved:
- Five sector progression and announcements.
- Existing hazards, projectiles, checkpoints, stamina and energy shards.
- Two-second gravity-flip cooldown.
- Menus, audio, scoring, lives, victory and game-over flow.

Controls remain:
- A / D: strafe
- SPACE: jump
- Q: gravity flip (2-second cooldown)
- W or SHIFT: boost while stamina remains
- S or CTRL: brake

Testing note:
- ZIP archive integrity was checked.
- Static source checks were performed.
- An actual Godot 4.7.2 engine launch / gameplay test was not available in the build environment.
