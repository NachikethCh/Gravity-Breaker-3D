GRAVITY BREAKER v8 — SECTOR PROGRESSION UPDATE

Base: v7 Dark Graph Grid. The existing tunnel/grid palette, gravity controls,
2-second gravity cooldown, stamina boost/braking, player model, menus, audio,
projectile telegraphs, scoring persistence, lives and 1500 m track are retained.

1. Five themed sectors, each 300 m long:
   - 000–300 m: Training Zone
   - 300–600 m: Laser Matrix
   - 600–900 m: Mechanical Sector
   - 900–1200 m: Reactor Core
   - 1200–1500 m: Final Gauntlet

2. Sector presentation:
   - Five coloured, non-collidable entry frames are generated under Level.
   - A sector announcement and short subtitle appear at each transition.
   - The HUD identifies the current sector.
   - Existing checkpoints remain at 300, 600, 900 and 1200 m.

3. Sector-specific hazard pacing:
   - Training: fewer early spikes and laser gates with more space to learn.
   - Laser Matrix: tighter laser spacing and faster two-axis gate movement.
   - Mechanical Sector: denser moving spikes and a sequence of crushers/fans.
   - Reactor Core: energy mines, machinery and faster enemy attacks.
   - Final Gauntlet: denser moving lasers, larger/moving hazards and combined
     crusher/fan/mine patterns.

4. Zone-based enemy projectile ramp:
   - Training begins at a slower cadence and speed.
   - Interval and speed step up at every sector boundary and ramp further
     inside each sector.
   - Existing single-shot, three-shot burst, five-lane spread and warning
     telegraphs remain active.

5. Energy shards:
   - Five rotating collectible shards, one per sector, appear on different
     running surfaces.
   - Collecting a shard restores up to 30 stamina and awards 250 bonus points.
   - Bonus points are tracked separately so they survive score recomputation.
   - Collected shards stay hidden after checkpoint respawns, but return on a
     full retry/restart.

6. Final sequence:
   - The last sector combines all three special hazard families with a denser
     laser rhythm before the existing finish marker at 1500 m.
   - Victory summary now confirms that all five sectors were cleared.
