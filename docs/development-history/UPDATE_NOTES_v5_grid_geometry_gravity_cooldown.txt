GRAVITY BREAKER v5 — GEOMETRY GRID + GRAVITY RECHARGE

VISUAL CHANGE
- Replaced the v4 repeating texture overlay with four actual ArrayMesh grid surfaces.
- The grid is built from thin rectangular mesh strips over the floor, ceiling, left wall, and right wall.
- 1 m subdivisions form the graph-paper pattern; 2 m lines are stronger and every 10 m has a restrained blue-violet accent.
- Existing tunnel surface colours, scene lighting, and glow settings were not increased or brightened in this update.
- The four surfaces are batched as one mesh each rather than one node per grid line.

GAMEPLAY CHANGE
- Q gravity flip now has a 2.0 second cooldown after a successful flip.
- The cooldown is enforced inside the flip handler, not just shown in the HUD.
- The HUD displays GRAVITY RECHARGING and a refill bar; a premature Q press briefly displays GRAVITY LOCKED without stopping movement or replaying the flip effects.
- Cooldown resets on respawn/restart.

PRESERVED
- 1500 m course, checkpoints, hazards and their movement, projectiles and attack telegraphs, boost/brake stamina, menus, lives, scoring, saved best score, audio, gravity movement and camera rotation remain based on v4.

VALIDATION
- ZIP integrity checked.
- Static source checks performed for balanced delimiters, duplicate function definitions, expected call sites, and preserved project files.
- Godot 4.7.2 was not available in this environment, so this build has not been launched or runtime-tested in the engine. Test this ZIP in a separate folder before replacing the working v4 project.
