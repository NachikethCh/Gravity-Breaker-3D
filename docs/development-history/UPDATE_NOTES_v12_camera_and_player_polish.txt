GRAVITY BREAKER v12 — CAMERA & PLAYER EXPERIENCE POLISH

CAMERA VISIBILITY
- Replaced the fixed 6 m camera offset with a gravity-aware third-person chase camera.
- Camera now sits 10.5 m behind and 2.45 m above the active surface.
- Look-ahead grows from 22 m to 36 m as running speed rises/boost is used.
- Field of view adapts smoothly from 82 to 89 degrees while boosting.
- Camera orientation and offset rotate with gravity across floor, walls and ceiling.
- Respawns snap the camera to the current checkpoint with the new framing.

GUARDIAN OBJECTIVE READABILITY
- Guardian cores and target rings enlarged for visibility.
- Core glow/light slightly strengthened without changing tunnel lighting.
- Guardian HUD now reports the distance to the next core.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
- Added dedicated How to Play and Credits pages to the title menu.
- Added How to Play access from the pause menu.
- Shortened the title-page text and made gameplay HUD information wrap across two lines.
- Shifted Guardian/attack-warning UI lower to reduce overlap with the status HUD.
- Existing gameplay logic, scoring, boss phases, projectiles, speed, stamina, checkpoints and audio assets are retained.

RELEASE STATUS
- This is still a test build. A Godot 4.7.2 import/launch, camera playtest on all four surfaces, Windows/Linux export, performance pass and audio redistribution clearance are required before public release.
