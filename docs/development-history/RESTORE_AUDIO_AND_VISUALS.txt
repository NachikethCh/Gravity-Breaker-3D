GRAVITY BREAKER — Audio & Visual Feedback Restoration

This update keeps the existing game structure and gameplay logic, including the title screen, PLAY/QUIT buttons, game-over and victory screens, HUD, movement, gravity switching, hazards, checkpoints, score saving, and the three enemy projectile patterns.

Restored audio (assets/audio/):
- ambient_loop.wav — background loop (starts when the scene loads)
- gravity_flip.wav — Q gravity switch
- projectile.wav — enemy projectile shot
- damage.wav — player hit
- game_over.wav — final life lost
- checkpoint.wav — checkpoint reached
- victory.wav — finish reached

Restored/added visual feedback:
- Expanding cyan ring + brief blue screen flash on gravity flip
- Expanding red ring + red screen flash when hit
- Green ring + green screen flash at checkpoints
- Green finish pulse/flash on victory
- Enabled WorldEnvironment glow for the existing emissive cyan/orange/red materials

Installation options:
1. Safest: extract this archive to a NEW folder, then open its project.godot in Godot.
2. To patch an existing copy: back it up first, replace scenes/player.gd and scenes/main.tscn, and keep the complete assets/audio folder from this archive.

The archive intentionally omits the generated .godot editor/import cache. Godot will rebuild that cache when the project is opened.

Runtime validation note: the source was statically inspected and the archive integrity checked, but it was not executed in Godot in this environment.
