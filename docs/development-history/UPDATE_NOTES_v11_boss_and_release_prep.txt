GRAVITY BREAKER v11 — TOWER GUARDIAN + RELEASE PREPARATION

Built on the user's confirmed working v10 progressive-speed project.

New gameplay:
- Procedural Tower Guardian encounter begins around 1280 m.
- Guardian visual assembled from original procedural mesh parts.
- Three exposed core targets are positioned on the floor, left wall and ceiling.
- Each core reduces Guardian integrity, grants +750 score and restores 18 stamina.
- Boss health and the next required surface are displayed in the HUD.
- The Guardian has a subtle idle hover/turn and phase announcements explain the next core target.
- Boss HUD now names the next core surface and shows the required A+Q direction for wall/ceiling targets.
- Controls explain A+Q to advance floor → left wall → ceiling, and D+Q to reverse.
- Final-sector projectiles become more frequent and faster as Guardian integrity falls.
- Reaching 1500 m before defeating the Guardian costs one life and returns the player to the last checkpoint; remaining cores persist.
- Defeating all three cores unlocks the normal 1500 m finish and victory screen.

Player-experience polish:
- ESC pause/resume overlay.
- Settings screen with master volume, music volume and fullscreen toggle.
- Settings persist with best score in user://gravity_breaker_save.cfg.
- Audio players, the run AnimationPlayer and feedback tweens stop while paused.
- Custom original project icon in SVG, PNG and Windows ICO formats.

Release preparation:
- Windows Desktop and Linux/X11 64-bit export presets added.
- README with export instructions and player controls.
- CREDITS.md includes known Sketchfab model attribution and CC BY 4.0.
- ASSET_LICENSE_AUDIT.md flags audio provenance as unresolved because the WAV files contain no embedded attribution metadata.
- RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md tracks full QA, export and publication work.

Preserved from v10:
- 1500 m course and all five sectors.
- Existing grid, sector gates, obstacles, energy shards and projectiles.
- 9-to-14 units/s cruise progression, 22 units/s boost cap, 4 units/s brake floor.
- Stamina system, 2-second gravity cooldown, scoring, checkpoints, lives, high score, menus and audio.

- The boss collision flow, pause UX, settings persistence and actual Windows/Linux export must be tested in Godot before public release.
