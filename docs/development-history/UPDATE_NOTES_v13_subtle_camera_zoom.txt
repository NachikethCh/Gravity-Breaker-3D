GRAVITY BREAKER v13 — Subtle Camera Zoom

This update is based on the tested v12 project. It makes a small camera-only adjustment:
- Chase camera distance: 9.0 m -> 8.3 m (slightly closer).
- Base camera FOV: 78 degrees -> 76 degrees (mild zoom-in).
- Boost FOV: 85 degrees -> 83.5 degrees (keeps the boost effect but avoids excessive widening).

The camera remains gravity-aware, retains its speed-sensitive look-ahead, and continues to widen gently during boost. No movement, gravity, stamina, hazard, boss, score, checkpoint, menu, or save-system logic was changed.

Testing note: ZIP integrity and source configuration are checked, but the final camera feel must be confirmed in Godot 4.7.2.
