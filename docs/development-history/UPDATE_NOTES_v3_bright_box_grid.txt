GRAVITY BREAKER — v3 BRIGHT BOX-GRID ENVIRONMENT

This is a visual-only correction on top of v2. Gameplay and hazard scripts are unchanged.

VISUAL CHANGES
- Raised the brightness and colour contrast of the four shaft base surfaces.
- Increased ambient illumination and slightly raised directional light energy.
- Replaced faint sparse markings with a real rectangular 2 m graph grid on the floor, ceiling, left wall, and right wall.
- Grid lines use thick, unshaded cyan with violet accent lines so they stay visible at a distance and on every gravity surface.
- Expanded alternating cyan/indigo modular tile plates to 9.75 m with narrow seams.
- Retained repeating bright wall ribs for depth and scale.
- Batched grid geometry with MultiMesh instances to limit draw calls.

UNCHANGED
- 1500 m course, checkpoints, hazards, projectiles, telegraphs, stamina boost/brake, scoring, audio, menus, respawn, victory, and game-over systems are unchanged.

TESTING
- Archive integrity checked.
- No Godot executable is available in the build environment, so this has not been launched in-engine.
