GRAVITY BREAKER v0.15.0 — Fullscreen and camera framing

- First launch after v0.15 defaults to fullscreen.
- Existing save files are migrated once: fullscreen is enabled and a migration flag is stored. After that, the player's Settings choice is respected.
- Reset Settings now restores fullscreen as the default.
- Camera moves 0.5 m closer (7.0 m behind the player).
- Camera FOV tightens slightly: 71.5 degrees normal, 79.5 degrees during boost.
- Speed-based look-ahead is retained to preserve hazard visibility.
- Verbose moving-hazard setup messages are limited to debug builds.
- Duplicate UIDs were removed from the two inactive legacy scene copies that caused startup/export warnings. Active scene UIDs are unchanged.
- Product metadata and documentation updated to 0.15.0.

No movement, stamina, gravity, hazard, score, checkpoint, Guardian, or audio gameplay logic was intentionally changed.
