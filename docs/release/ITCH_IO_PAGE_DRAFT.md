## Short description

Defy gravity and race through a 1500-metre futuristic tower. Flip between the floor, walls and ceiling, manage your boost stamina, survive five increasingly dangerous sectors, and destroy the Tower Guardian's exposed cores.

## About this game

GRAVITY BREAKER is a fast-paced, four-surface action runner. The tower is a 1500-metre gauntlet divided into five sectors, each introducing a tougher mix of moving lasers, spikes, crushers, rotating blades, energy mines and enemy projectiles.

Gravity is your main survival tool. Switch between the floor, left wall, ceiling and right wall, time your jumps, and choose when to spend stamina on a burst of speed. Your normal running pace increases as you climb deeper into the tower. Checkpoints give you a chance to recover, but the final challenge is waiting at the top.

The Tower Guardian activates near the end. Reach its exposed cores across the floor, left wall and ceiling, destroy all three, and then make it to the finish.

## Features

- 1500 m course divided into five themed sectors.
- Four-surface gravity switching with a two-second cooldown.
- Progressive running speed, stamina-based boost and braking.
- Moving lasers, spikes, crushers, rotating fans, energy mines and enemy projectiles.
- Checkpoints, collectibles, score and saved best score.
- A three-stage Tower Guardian core encounter.

## Controls

- **A / D:** Strafe; hold with Q to select gravity-flip direction.
- **Space:** Jump.
- **Q:** Flip gravity.
- **W / Shift:** Boost while stamina remains.
- **S / Ctrl:** Brake.
- **Esc:** Pause / resume.
