# GRAVITY BREAKER — Player Quick-Start

## Your objective

Run through five sectors of the 1500 m tower, survive the hazards, activate checkpoints, and destroy the Tower Guardian's three cores before crossing the finish.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| A / D | Strafe left / right across the current surface |
| Space | Jump |
| Q | Flip gravity to an adjacent surface (2-second cooldown) |
| A + Q | Advance: floor → left wall → ceiling → right wall |
| D + Q | Reverse the surface order |
| W / Shift | Boost while stamina remains |
| S / Ctrl | Brake; release to return to cruise speed |
| Esc | Pause / resume |
| R | Restart after the run ends |

The character auto-runs. Cruise speed rises from 9 to 14 units/s over the course. Boost can reach 22 units/s, but consumes stamina. Stamina recovers when boost is released. Braking slows the runner to a minimum of 4 units/s.

## Gravity switching

Press Q together with A or D to choose the direction of the next surface. Wait for the full 2-second cooldown before attempting another flip. The camera rotates with the player.

## The five sectors

1. Training Zone — learn movement and gravity.
2. Laser Matrix — moving beams and tighter timing.
3. Mechanical Sector — crushers and rotating blades.
4. Reactor Core — mines and heavier projectile combinations.
5. Final Gauntlet — mixed hazards and the Tower Guardian.

## Tower Guardian

The Guardian encounter begins near 1280 m. Destroy the three exposed cores in order: floor at 1328 m, left wall at 1392 m, and ceiling at 1458 m. Follow the HUD's `NEXT` surface and `CORE IN` distance. Each core grants bonus score and stamina. If the finish is reached while the Guardian remains active, a life is lost and the runner respawns at the latest checkpoint; destroyed cores remain destroyed.

## Tips

- Use normal running speed to line up with targets; boost only when the path is clear.
- Brake before a difficult core or dense hazard pattern.
- Watch the attack-warning text and target rings before enemy projectiles launch.
- Checkpoints preserve course progress, so use them to learn later sectors.

## Display

The game starts in fullscreen by default. Open Settings and disable **Fullscreen Mode** to return to a window; the choice is saved for later launches.
